Basic Rules & Guidelines:

1. Be Patient!

If you commission me please don't expect an immediate response. Most of my commissions usually take 5 (FIVE) or more days to finish.

2. Strictly no NSFW

Pretty self explanatory don't you think? Anyways, if you ask me to add/make nsfw content to my commissions I will decline immediately

3. Do NOT feed my work to AI

Please don't feed my work to AI. Don't feed my code nor my renders to AI.

4. Please communicate properly

Always remember that communication is key! If you don't communicate with me properly the result of your commissions can and will come out wonky/not what you envisioned.

Payment Method:

My payment method will be GCash! Don't pay right away however, pay once I'm done with your commission.

Do note: You will be paying me before I show the final product. This is just a precaution hope you unserstand!

How long does a commission take?

A commission should usually take 4-5 (FOUR to FIVE) days but, if I'm busy it might take longer than that. I will give you updates on your comission to make sure the final product is satisfactory.
